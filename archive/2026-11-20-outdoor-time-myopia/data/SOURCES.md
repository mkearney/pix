# Sources

No trial-level microdata were released for these studies, so every value is transcribed from the published abstracts, saved verbatim in `pubmed_abstracts.txt` (PubMed E-utilities, fetched 2026-11-20).

- `cochrane2024_incidence.csv`: pooled incidence of myopia with and without outdoor-time interventions, risk ratios with 95% CIs, numbers of studies and participants and GRADE certainty at one, two and three years. Kido A, Miyake M, Watanabe N. Interventions to increase time spent outdoors for preventing incidence and progression of myopia in children. *Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews* 2024, Issue 6, CD013549. doi:10.1002/14651858.CD013549.pub2 (PMID 38864362), abstract, Main results.
- `trials.csv`: cumulative incidence by arm in the two largest trials. He M et al., *JAMA* 314:1142-1148 (2015), doi:10.1001/jama.2015.10803 (PMID 26372583): cases and eligible children per arm, and the risk difference with 95% CI. He X et al., *Ophthalmology* 129:1245-1254 (2022), doi:10.1016/j.ophtha.2022.06.024 (PMID 35779695): unadjusted two-year incidence per arm and adjusted incidence risk ratios with 95% CIs.
- `pubmed_abstracts.txt`: the abstracts above plus Cao et al. 2020 (PMID 31430758) and Xiong et al. 2017 (PMID 28251836), kept as the record of transcribed text.
