Younger relative age was associated with ADHD diagnosis and medication, with RR of 1.38 (95% CI 1.36, 1.52) and 1.28 (95% CI 1.21, 1.36) respectively. This means that children who are younger relative to their peers within the same school year are 38% more likely to receive an ADHD diagnosis and 28% more likely to be prescribed ADHD medications.
