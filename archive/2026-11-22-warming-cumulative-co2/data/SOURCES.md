# Sources

- `gcb_cumulative_co2_world.csv`: cumulative CO2 emissions from fossil fuels, industry and land-use change, World, 1850-2025, in tonnes. Our World in Data grapher `cumulative-co2-including-land` (https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/cumulative-co2-including-land.csv?v=1&csvType=full&useColumnShortNames=true), built on the Global Carbon Budget (Global Carbon Project). Downloaded 2026-11-22; the World rows only are kept.
- `hadcrut5_global_annual.csv`: HadCRUT.5.1.0.0 global annual mean surface temperature anomaly (relative to 1961-1990) with 2.5% and 97.5% confidence limits, Met Office Hadley Centre and Climatic Research Unit. https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/hadcrut5/data/HadCRUT.5.1.0.0/analysis/diagnostics/HadCRUT.5.1.0.0.analysis.summary_series.global.annual.csv, downloaded 2026-11-22. `analysis.py` re-bases it to 1850-1900 and drops 2026, an incomplete year.
- `ipcc_ar6_wg1_spm_D1.txt`: text of paragraphs D.1.1 and D.1.2 of the IPCC AR6 Working Group I Summary for Policymakers (2021), extracted from https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGI_SPM.pdf on 2026-11-22. Source of the assessed relationship (0.45°C per 1000 GtCO2, likely range 0.27 to 0.63) and the 1850-2019 total of 2390 ± 240 GtCO2.
