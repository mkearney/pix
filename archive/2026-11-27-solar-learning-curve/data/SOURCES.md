# Sources

- `solar-pv-prices-vs-cumulative-capacity.csv`: Our World in Data grapher download, https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/solar-pv-prices-vs-cumulative-capacity.csv?v=1&csvType=full&useColumnShortNames=true, downloaded 2026-11-27. Module price in constant 2025 US$ per watt (Nemet 2009 for 1975–2003; Farmer & Lafond 2016 for 2004–2009; IRENA Renewable Power Generation Costs 2024 and 2025 for 2010 on) and cumulative capacity in megawatts (Nemet 2009; IRENA Renewable Energy Statistics).
- `solar-pv-prices-vs-cumulative-capacity.metadata.json`: the same chart's metadata, with full source descriptions.
- `rubin_2015_excerpt.txt`: Table 1 row and sentences on solar PV learning rates from Rubin et al., Energy Policy 86:198-218 (2015), doi:10.1016/j.enpol.2015.06.011, extracted from the author-hosted PDF at cmu.edu, downloaded 2026-11-27.
