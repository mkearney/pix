# Leads

Candidate findings that look settled and chartable. A lead is a starting point, not a fact:
before using one, read the papers, confirm the numbers and run the full evidence check in
`CLAUDE.md`. Delete a lead once it's used. Add strong candidates as you find them, keeping
the list across many fields.

Each line: the finding · finding type · where to start · what the chart could be.

## Health & medicine

- **HPV vaccination protects most when given young.** moderator · Falcaro et al., Lancet 2021
  (England); Lei et al., NEJM 2020 (Sweden); Palmer et al., JNCI 2024 (Scotland) ·
  reduction in cervical cancer by age at vaccination, with intervals.
- **Mortality risk levels off below 10,000 daily steps, earlier in older adults.** dose-response,
  association wording · Paluch et al., Lancet Public Health 2022; Ding et al., Lancet Public
  Health 2025 · hazard ratio against daily steps with a band, split by age if the data allow.
- **Lowering blood pressure helps whatever the starting level.** moderator (absence of one) ·
  Blood Pressure Lowering Treatment Trialists' Collaboration, Lancet 2021; Ettehad et al.,
  Lancet 2016 · effect per 5 mmHg by baseline blood pressure band, a forest plot.
- **Each step down in LDL cholesterol cuts heart attacks and strokes in proportion.**
  dose-response · Cholesterol Treatment Trialists' Collaboration, Lancet 2010; European
  Atherosclerosis Society consensus, Eur Heart J 2017 · trials plotted by LDL reduction
  against risk reduction.
- **Cutting salt lowers blood pressure most in older people and those with hypertension.**
  moderator · Huang et al., BMJ 2020 (dose-response meta-analysis of trials); Sacks et al.,
  NEJM 2001 (DASH-Sodium) · dose-response lines split by subgroup.
- **Exercise lowers blood pressure, and isometric training lowers it most.** comparison ·
  Edwards et al., BJSM 2023 (network meta-analysis); Naci et al., BJSM 2019 · forest plot by
  exercise type.
- **Alcohol raises breast cancer risk with every drink.** dose-response · Collaborative Group
  on Hormonal Factors in Breast Cancer, Br J Cancer 2002; IARC Monographs · relative risk by
  daily grams of alcohol.
- **Weight and mortality follow a J-curve that is steeper in younger adults.** moderator,
  association wording · Global BMI Mortality Collaboration, Lancet 2016; Bhaskaran et al.,
  Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2018 · hazard ratio by BMI, small multiples by age.
- **GLP-1 drugs cut body weight far more than placebo in large trials.** effect · Wilding et al., NEJM
  2021 (STEP 1); Jastreboff et al., NEJM 2022 (SURMOUNT-1); Lincoff et al., NEJM 2023
  (SELECT) · weight change over time, drug against placebo.
- **Folic acid fortification cut neural tube defects.** effect · Williams et al., MMWR 2015;
  Atta et al., Am J Public Health 2016 (global meta-analysis) · prevalence before and after
  fortification, or fortified against unfortified countries.

## Children, learning & the mind

- **The best gap between study sessions grows with how long you need to remember.**
  moderator · Cepeda et al., Psychological Bulletin 2006 (meta-analysis); Cepeda et al.,
  Psychological Science 2008 · recall against gap, one curve per test delay.
- **Testing yourself beats rereading.** effect · Rowland, Psychological Bulletin 2014;
  Adesope et al., Review of Educational Research 2017 · effect sizes by condition.

## Climate, energy & environment

- **Solar module prices fall by a steady share with each doubling of installed capacity.**
  dose-response · Way et al., Joule 2022; IRENA cost reports · log-log price against
  cumulative capacity (OWID).
- **Cold kills more people than heat.** dose-response · Gasparrini et al., Lancet 2015 (384
  locations); Zhao et al., Lancet Planetary Health 2021 (750 locations) · relative risk
  against temperature percentile, with attributable deaths.
- **Fine-particle pollution raises mortality, with no safe threshold.** dose-response ·
  Burnett et al., PNAS 2018 (Global Exposure Mortality Model); Chen and Hoek, Environment
  International 2020 · hazard ratio against PM2.5 concentration.
- **The ozone layer is healing after the Montreal Protocol.** effect · WMO/UNEP Scientific
  Assessment of Ozone Depletion 2022; NASA Ozone Watch data · ozone-depleting substances and
  the Antarctic ozone hole over time.

## Global health & development

- **Bed nets did most of the work in Africa's malaria decline.** comparison · Bhatt et al.,
  Nature 2015; Pryce et al., Cochrane 2018 · cases averted by intervention, stacked over time.
  Checked 2026-11-25 and set aside: the 68/22/10 split rests on one modelling study (the WHO
  and later work reuse the same group's model), so it lacks independent corroboration.
- **Childhood vaccines have prevented about 150 million deaths in 50 years.** comparison
  (modelled, label it) · Shattock et al., Lancet 2024 · deaths averted by vaccine over time.
- **Child mortality fell with mothers' years of schooling.** association · Gakidou et al.,
  Lancet 2010 · child mortality against mothers' education across countries.

## Economics, society & safety

- **Life satisfaction rises with the log of income, without a plateau.** association ·
  Stevenson and Wolfers, AER Papers & Proceedings 2013; Deaton, J Econ Perspect 2008;
  Killingsworth, Kahneman and Mellers, PNAS 2023 · country averages against GDP per person
  on a log axis.
- **Road deaths rise with roughly the fourth power of average speed.** dose-response · Elvik,
  Accident Analysis & Prevention 2013 (Power Model); Nilsson 2004 · change in fatal crashes
  against change in mean speed.

## Natural world

- **Larger animals burn less energy per gram.** dose-response · Kleiber 1932; White and
  Seymour, PNAS 2003 (exponent debate: claim only the settled part) · log-log metabolic rate
  against body mass (AnAge data).
